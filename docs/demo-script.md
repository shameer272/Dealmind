# DealMind Hackathon Demo Script (3-5 Minutes)

## Goal
Demonstrate how Hindsight persistent memory transforms generic AI sales advice into high-precision, deal-winning intelligence.

---

### Step 1: The Problem (0:00 - 0:45)
- **Presenter**: "B2B sales deals take months. Sales reps handle dozens of accounts simultaneously, forgetting crucial budget limits, architectural objections, and promises made weeks ago."
- **Action**: Open DealMind Dashboard. Point out active pipeline, health scores, and upcoming meetings.

---

### Step 2: The Flagship Deal Story — Acme Technologies (0:45 - 1:30)
- **Presenter**: "Let's explore our flagship deal: Acme Enterprise AI Platform. Over the last 45 days, we've had 10 interactions with Sarah Jenkins (VP Eng) and Vikram Rao (Lead Architect)."
- **Action**: Navigate to Acme Deal Page.
- **Highlight**:
  - Show the **Memory Timeline** (Day 45: Need identified -> Day 40: Budget capped at ₹10L -> Day 30: Salesforce competitor -> Day 25: CRM Webhook latency objection -> Day 20: SOC2 security concern).
  - Show the **Customer Memory Explorer** highlighting structured facts categorized by Hindsight.

---

### Step 3: The Wow Moment — "Why Memory Matters" (1:30 - 2:30)
- **Presenter**: "Here is the core reason we built DealMind on Hindsight."
- **Action**: Click the **Memory Impact** comparison tab.
- **Contrast**:
  - **Without Memory**: Generic AI output ("Ask customer what their budget and pain points are").
  - **With Hindsight**: "Acme has already confirmed ₹10L budget in Interaction 2. Primary objection is CRM Webhook latency (raised 4 times). Competitor is Salesforce Einstein. You promised SOC2 audit report."
- **Presenter**: "Point out how every single bullet links directly to its historical Hindsight memory source with explainability!"

---

### Step 4: Live Action — "Prepare Me" for Meeting (2:30 - 3:30)
- **Presenter**: "I have an alignment meeting with Acme tomorrow morning. Let's ask DealMind to prepare me."
- **Action**: Click **"Prepare for Meeting"**.
- **Result**:
  - Customer Snapshot & Care-Abouts.
  - Previous Objections (CRM Webhook sync, Pricing).
  - Competitor Counter-Strategy (vs Salesforce).
  - Recommended Talking Points (Demonstrate sub-50ms sync latency, confirm receipt of SOC2 pack).
  - Suggested Next Best Action.

---

### Step 5: Ingesting a New Interaction & Memory Growth (3:30 - 4:15)
- **Presenter**: "Watch what happens in real time when we add a new meeting note."
- **Action**: Click **"Add Interaction"**, enter note: "Vikram confirmed sandbox passed! Next step is board review."
- **Result**: DealMind analyzes the note, extracts new facts, updates Hindsight memory bank, and adjusts Deal Health score.
- **Action**: Open **Learning Curve** to view memory growth chart across the 10 interactions.

---

### Step 6: Wrap-up (4:15 - 5:00)
- **Presenter**: "The LLM reasons. Hindsight remembers. PostgreSQL stores state. The agent orchestrates. Thank you!"
